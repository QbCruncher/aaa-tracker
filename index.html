What the AI Agent Avalanche is

Today’s AI agents still feel different from each other. One is good at email, another can control your phone, a third lives on your computer and remembers everything. That won’t last.

Every major company (and several open-source projects) is adding the same set of abilities as fast as they can: controlling your phone and computer, logging into sites for you, reading and sending messages, handling payments, staying on in the background, and remembering your life across all of it. Once an agent has all of these, there is almost nothing left that only a human can do online.

We call this the AI Agent Avalanche. The products will keep different names and brands, but the capability gap between them will close until they are effectively the same product — a full-access personal agent. The only remaining difference will be which one you trust with your life.

What “full access” looks like:

  • Uses your phone and computer the way you do
  • Knows your passwords and can log in (with or without asking)
  • Reads and replies to email, texts, and messages
  • Pays for things, orders food, books travel
  • Remembers your preferences, people, and history
  • Keeps working while you’re asleep or offline
